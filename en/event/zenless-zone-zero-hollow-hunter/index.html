HOW WE SCORE / SCORING

Ignore the noise.
Judge these five things.

We try to break down “this feels fun” into something discussable. Each axis is scored 1–10, then calibrated around a midpoint of 5: 5→8 is easier, 8→9 harder. Manual up/down clicks follow a separate 0–10, 100-step curve and can reach 10 or 0. The overall score is the average of the five calibrated axes, rounded to one decimal. “Time” measures time cost; a higher score means less time. No hidden weights, no editorial adjustment.
